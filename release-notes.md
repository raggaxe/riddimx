RiddimX 0.5.0 — one piece of hardware: a cleaner mixer, five centre pages, set timer, notes and a new Drops maker

Look and feel
- Deck A, the mixer and deck B now read as one unit: one surface, thin seams, no boxes in boxes.
- Machined knobs with a printed scale of dots that light up as you turn; raised keys with rounded corners.
- Deck colours are red (A) and blue (B) by default; anything switched on lights up yellow.
- The crossfader is bigger and takes the whole bar; the transition keys are taller.
- Simpler VU meters, a finer pitch scale on both sides of the fader, and a padlock on KEY (key lock).

Centre pages (no more scrolling)
- MIXER · DUB · FX · DECK DUB · TAPE · MIC.
- FX: deck A | master | deck B, each with its own effect, THROW and how much of each stem goes into AUX 1 and AUX 2.
- DECK DUB: the echo of the decks' DUB button (presets, time, feedback, tone, hold / latch…).
- TAPE: the master tape echo. MIC: the live mic (talk, presets, echo, colour).
- DUB fits on one page: big DROP / FIRE / FX, sound pads, HOLD / LATCH.

New
- Set countdown: click the clock, pick the length of your set, START. It sits on the top bar (amber in the last 10 minutes, red at the end) or floats as a widget you can move.
- NOTES on the top bar: write your set plan; lines starting with "- " tick themselves when that track has played. + ON AIR adds the track playing, + TIME adds a time stamp.
- Drops, reworked in three steps (Say it · Sound · Use it), with MAKE & PLAY right under the text.
- Cut a drop from a track on a deck, from the library or from any audio file — from the whole mix or one stem (vocals, drums, bass, music).

Renamed and tidied
- The bottom "Mic" tab is now "Voice" (Live mic + Drops); "Plugin" is now "FX". The Sampler shows only its pads.
- Every bottom tab (Sampler, Dub Box, Tape echo, Voice, FX, Master, Spotify) follows the same clean layout.
- Transitions: the bar under a transition key empties when it ends, and the deck that went out goes back to its cue.

Windows
- The Windows version (RiddimX-win32-x64.zip) is built from the same code: unzip and open RiddimX.exe.
